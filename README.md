# Hi, I'm Brando Fraiz 👋

**Aeronautical & Computer Engineer** · Scientific software · Air navigation · Autonomous systems

I build simulation tools to analyze complex problems in air navigation and operational safety. My background combines aeronautical engineering, software development and mathematical modeling. I care about understanding systems end to end: the operational problem, the interfaces, the architecture, the constraints, and how a solution is validated under realistic conditions.

## 🛩️ What I'm doing now

**Researcher at the Air Navigation System Lab, ITACA Institute, Universitat Politècnica de València**

- **Collision Risk Model (CRM):** a C++ simulator that evaluates collision risk between manned and unmanned aircraft, and the effect of different air traffic management services on that risk. I work across the whole stack: mathematical and statistical models, software architecture, Monte Carlo experiments, and methods for estimating extremely rare events.
- **U-space & very-low-level airspace:** contributions to the European projects **DAEDALUS** and **U-AGREE** on dynamic airspace management, conflict resolution and risk assessment.
- Technical and scientific documentation, integration of contributions from multiple partners, and research publications.

Currently exploring UAS flight simulation architectures, autopilot integration and terrain processing with GDAL.
